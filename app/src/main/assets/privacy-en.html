Privacy Notice

This app has no developer server and contains no ads, analytics, or crash reporting. The developer never receives your photos, filenames, or account tokens.

Photo filenames, folders, sizes, content checksums, and OneDrive links are stored only in this app's private directory. To detect moved files and identical copies, the app reads photos and videos visible in internal storage, and only creates new managed links for the folders you select.

Full SHA-256 digests of photos and videos are computed on your device. Before cleanup, the cloud file is streamed from Microsoft's download URL for verification without saving another copy or uploading local photos or digests to the developer.

Sign-in is handled by the Microsoft Authentication Library (MSAL). Tokens are managed by Microsoft's library and are never displayed or logged by this app. App data backup is disabled; never upload app private data or sign-in caches to public repositories.

Microsoft requires delegated Files.ReadWrite scope, which covers read and write access because no separate delete-only scope exists. This app's business logic implements only queries and moving items to the recycle bin; there is no upload, overwrite, or permanent delete operation.

Network access is used for Microsoft sign-in, OneDrive folder listing, content verification, and moving items to the recycle bin. When you tap links to open the guide, repository, or OneDrive web pages, your browser connects to those sites.

You can disconnect this app, revoke authorization in your Microsoft account, or uninstall the app to remove local records. Uninstalling does not delete OneDrive photos.
